GitHub — release / issues

1. When you tag a release, does a Features / Fixes-style update reach Slack (or email) without someone rewriting the compare view by hand?

2. Do merged PRs for a release stay grouped with authors + links somewhere durable (not just “scroll GitHub”)?

3. Do issue/PR labels (or keywords) route customer-facing or billing noise somewhere other than a single firehose channel?

4. Is there a clear human-review step before anything customer-facing is posted from an automation?

Slack — handoffs / triage

5. Can the team find “what shipped last week” in Slack in under a minute without asking the founder?

6. When support or “is X shipped?” hits Slack, does it land in a known channel/owner instead of whoever’s loudest?

7. Do bot/automation posts include a clear approve / edit / reject (or equivalent) gate before filing issues or sending externally?

Inbox — email handoffs

8. Do recurring inbound questions (pricing, how-to, feature asks) get a draft reply from known facts — not a blank cursor every time?

9. When eng needs a customer email, is there a triage path that isn’t “founder copy-pastes into Slack/GitHub by hand” as the default?

10. Do reply drafts (if any) refuse to invent prices, dates, or impact numbers the message didn’t state? (If you have no drafts, answer No.)
