Reply draft — inbound email → founder voice → human approval

This is a walkthrough of one sample run: a pricing ask arrives by email, a reply is drafted in the founder’s voice with deterministic templates and guardrails, and an approval card is shown. Everything below comes from fixture files. No live mailbox was used, and nothing gets sent until a person copies the draft into their own mail client.

  • Fixture: fixtures/pricing-email.json
  • Voice: fixtures/founder-voice.json
  • Deterministic templates, no LLM
  • Dry-run: nothing sent
  • Human approval required

These chips are illustrative; they don’t do anything on this page. Simulate a decision with --decision approve|edit|reject. Even approve only prints a hand-off — you send from your own client. Optional --post-slack posts the approval card only (never the email).
